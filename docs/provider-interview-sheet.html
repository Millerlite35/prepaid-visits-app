Provider call sheet

About 15 minutes. Listen first, demo second, ask for the pilot every time.

1 How they work now most of the call

  1. Do you sell packages or prepaid visits now? If not, have you thought about it? What stopped you?
    YesThought about itUsed toNo
  2. Walk me through the last package you sold. How did they pay, and how did you track it?
  3. How do you know how many visits a client has left?
  4. What's the most annoying part of that?
  5. Has it ever gone wrong (a disputed count, a forgotten visit, a refund)? What happened?
  6. About how many package clients? Typical package price?
